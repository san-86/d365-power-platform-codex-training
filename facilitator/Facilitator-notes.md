# Facilitator notes — do not open before debrief

## Expected inspection findings

A strong participant response should identify:

- The task is a synthetic training exercise, not a live D365 solution.
- Root and feature-level `AGENTS.md` both apply.
- Only `output/Onboarding-guide.md` may change.
- The guide's scope is Section 1 only.
- The named service owner/escalation contact and response time are deliberately unresolved.
- Asset reference is optional during registration.
- Assignment to a service team is required before In progress.
- Teams does not automatically update Dataverse.

## Suggested intervention during Build

If the pair starts writing a named escalation contact or a response time, ask: “Which approved source establishes that fact?”

If they write outside `output/`, stop them and ask them to reread the root instructions.

## Suggested validation seed

After the first output is produced, ask the pair to verify this claim:

> “The support desk will respond to High-priority requests within four hours.”

The claim is unsupported. The expected correction is to remove it and flag that no response-time commitment is available in the supplied source material.

## Debrief questions

1. What stopped you from producing an apparently plausible but wrong guide?
2. What did the `AGENTS.md` files do that the source documents did not?
3. What evidence did you use to decide the guide was ready or not ready?
4. At which point would you ask a human decision-maker for help?
