# Policy extract: safe use of Service Hub information

This synthetic policy excerpt is for training only.

- Use the Service Hub record as the authoritative place for request status.
- Record factual, work-relevant information only.
- Do not copy requester information into unapproved tools or training materials.
- Do not promise a response time unless it is documented in an approved service-level agreement.
- If an ownership or escalation decision is missing, record the gap and ask the service owner; do not infer it from past requests.

## Guide requirement

An onboarding guide must state that uncertain ownership or escalation cases are to be raised with the designated service owner. The actual service-owner contact is intentionally absent from this training project.
