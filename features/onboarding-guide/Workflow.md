# Workflow: coordinator support process

## Status values

| Status | Meaning | Who may set it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New | The request has been registered and awaits triage or assignment. | Coordinator |
| In progress | A responsible service team has been assigned and work has started. | Coordinator or service team lead |
| Waiting for requester | The team needs information from the requester. | Service team lead |
| Resolved | The work is complete and the proposed resolution is recorded. | Service team lead |
| Closed | The request is administratively closed after resolution. | Service team lead |

## Registration workflow

1. Coordinator records the request and confirms the title, requester e-mail, category and priority.
2. Coordinator adds the asset reference if known; this is optional at this point.
3. Coordinator assigns the responsible service team.
4. The request remains **New** until a responsible team begins work.
5. The request may be marked **In progress** only after the responsible team is assigned.
6. The creation-time Power Automate flow sends one automatic confirmation.

## Escalation rule

When the correct owner is uncertain, the coordinator must raise the issue with the designated service owner. The name and contact route for that owner are unresolved in this training project; do not invent them.
